KPI dashboards

Data models & measures

Structured semantic models, SQL-sourced data, and DAX measures designed for dependable analysis.

  • Extract and validate source data with SQL
  • Shape and optimize transformations in Power Query

A strong report begins below the canvas. This work focuses on model relationships, calculation logic, naming, and refresh-aware transformations that make future reporting easier to maintain.

Reporting automation

Workflow improvements that reduce repetitive reporting steps and support consistent delivery.

  • Map manual reporting handoffs
  • Automate repeatable actions with Power Automate
  • Standardize reporting outputs in Excel and Power BI

Automation is applied where it removes avoidable effort: moving information, triggering recurring steps, and keeping reporting routines consistent while preserving clear points for human review.
